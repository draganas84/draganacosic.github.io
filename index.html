About me

I speak engineer and customer.

I earned an MSc in electrical and computer engineering, then went into marketing. It turns out, most tech companies don't have a product problem, they have a translation problem. For 16+ years, I've been the translator.

Paid media taught me optimization: I made a struggling account over 7x more profitable. Agencies taught me B2B, from content campaigns for U.S. clients to localization projects for Microsoft. I learned growth marketing at a SaaS startup, tripling blog traffic and revenue.

Then I moved closer to the product. At NOVELIC, I sat between engineers and OEMs who needed to understand why mmWave beats PIR technology. I ran go-to-market strategy for the company's first sensor product, from competitor analysis and content strategy to exhibiting at international trade shows. At Epic Games, I managed a €250K budget, negotiated 50% vendor savings, and collaborated with partners like Sony and Kojima Productions.

I like work where accuracy matters. I read the datasheet before I write the headline, I ask engineers the annoying questions, and I don't publish claims I can't back up. Lately, I've been building that same discipline into AI workflows with Claude Code, so speed doesn't cost credibility.

I'm looking for my next role, leading marketing for products or services that deserve a bigger audience.
